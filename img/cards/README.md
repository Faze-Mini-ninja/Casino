# Playing card art

The card faces and back in this folder are from **SVG-cards** (npm `svg-cards` 4.0.2,
https://github.com/htdebeer/SVG-cards), a fork of SVG-cards 2.0.1 by **David Bellot**
(http://svg-cards.sourceforge.net/). Licensed under the GNU LGPL 2.1 (see `LICENSE-LGPL-2.1.txt`).

The files here are the package's unmodified 2× PNG renders, converted to WebP for size.
